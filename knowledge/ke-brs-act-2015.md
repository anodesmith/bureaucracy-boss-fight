## Summary

Establishes the Business Registration Service (BRS) and gives it the mandate over
the registration of companies, partnerships and firms, and of persons carrying on
business under a business name. **It authorises the Service to charge fees but does
not state any amount.** Fee amounts are delegated to subsidiary rules.

### C-BRS-01 VERIFIED
- **claim**: The Business Registration Service is responsible, under the general supervision of the Cabinet Secretary, for implementing policies and laws relating to the registration of companies, partnerships and firms, and of individuals and corporations carrying on business under a business name.
- **locator**: section 4(1)
- **quote**: "The Service shall, under the general supervision of the Cabinet Secretary, be responsible for the implementation of policies, laws and other matters relating to the registration of Companies, partnerships and firms, individuals and corporations carrying on business under a business name, bankruptcy, hire-purchase, and security rights."
- **topics**: registration, BRS, mandate, attorney-general, registrar

### C-BRS-02 VERIFIED
- **claim**: The Business Registration Service is legally empowered to charge fees for any service it performs.
- **locator**: section 4(2)(f)
- **quote**: "charge fees for any service performed by the Service"
- **topics**: fees, charging, BRS

### C-BRS-03 VERIFIED
- **claim**: The Cabinet Secretary may make regulations for the better carrying into effect of the Act, so operational detail such as fee schedules sits in subsidiary legislation rather than in the Act itself.
- **locator**: section 30
- **quote**: "The Cabinet Secretary may make regulations generally for the better carrying into effect of the provisions of this Act."
- **topics**: regulations, subsidiary-legislation, fees

### C-BRS-04 VERIFIED
- **claim**: The Service's headquarters is in the capital city and it must establish branches in every County in Kenya to ensure reasonable access to its services.
- **locator**: section 3(3)
- **quote**: "The headquarters of the Service shall be in the capital city but the Service shall establish branches in every County in Kenya to ensure reasonable access of its services."
- **topics**: offices, counties, access, branches

### C-BRS-05 VERIFIED
- **claim**: It is an offence to knowingly submit false or misleading information to the Service.
- **locator**: section 29(1)(b)
- **quote**: "knowingly submit false or misleading information to the Service"
- **topics**: offence, false-information, penalty, compliance

### C-BRS-06 VERIFIED
- **claim**: On conviction for knowingly submitting false or misleading information to the Service, the penalty is a fine not exceeding five hundred thousand shillings, or imprisonment for a term not exceeding three years, or both.
- **locator**: section 29(2)
- **quote**: "is on conviction liable to a fine not exceeding five hundred thousand shillings or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three years or to both."
- **topics**: offence, penalty, fine, imprisonment

### C-BRS-07 VERIFIED
- **claim**: It is an offence to knowingly misrepresent anything in order to mislead a person acting under the Authority of the Service.
- **locator**: section 29(1)(c)
- **quote**: "knowingly misrepresent anything to mislead a person acting under the Authority of the Service under this Act"
- **topics**: offence, misrepresentation, fraud

### C-BRS-08 VERIFIED
- **claim**: On incorporation or registration of a business whose purpose is to undertake a manufacturing business, the Director-General must submit particulars of the business to the Kenya Bureau of Standards.
- **locator**: section 21A(1), inserted by the Business Laws (Amendment) Act, 2024 (Act No. 20 of 2024)
- **quote**: "The Director-General of the Business Registration Service shall immediately upon the incorporation or registration of a business whose purpose and object is to undertake manufacturing business submit particulars of the information on the business to the Kenya Bureau of Standards for purpose of section 5A of the Standards Act (Cap. 496)."
- **topics**: manufacturing, KEBS, standards, registration, food

### C-BRS-09 VERIFIED
- **claim**: The Act was published in Kenya Gazette Vol. CXVII-No. 101 on 18 September 2015, assented to on 11 September 2015 and commenced on 6 November 2015.
- **locator**: long title block
- **quote**: "Published in Kenya Gazette Vol. CXVII-No. 101 on 18 September 2015 ... Assented to on 11 September 2015 ... Commenced on 6 November 2015"
- **topics**: provenance, gazette, citation, commencement
