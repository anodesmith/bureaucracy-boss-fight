## Summary

If you search the open web for the cost of registering a Kenyan business name,
you will find at least six different numbers, no two of which agree, and you will
find them presented as fact. This document exists so that the Game Master's
EVIDENCE AGENT can show you *that* the conflict exists, rather than silently
picking one. This is the single most important UNKNOWN in the mission.

The underlying reason for the conflict is knowable and is recorded separately as
C-FEE-08: both governing Acts delegate the actual amounts to subsidiary rules
that this knowledge base does not contain. The amounts are knowable in principle.
They are simply not known *here*.

### C-FEE-08 INFERRED
- **claim**: A published, authoritative statement of the business name registration fee exists somewhere in Kenyan subsidiary legislation, and is simply absent from this knowledge base.
- **derivation**: Both governing Acts delegate fee-setting rather than stating amounts. C-RBN-09 (Registration of Business Names Act s.22(a)) provides that the Cabinet Secretary "may make rules ... for prescribing (a) the fees to be paid to the Registrar under this Act", and C-RBN-17 and C-RBN-06 establish that the "prescribed fee" is the operative charge. C-BRS-03 and C-BRS-02 do the same for the Business Registration Service. Therefore an amount, if it exists, is fixed in rules made under one of these Acts rather than in the Acts themselves.
- **locator**: derived from C-RBN-09, C-RBN-06, C-BRS-02, C-BRS-03
- **quote**: ""
- **confidence**: high
- **unknown_reason**: The inference establishes that a source exists. It does not establish what the source says. The amount remains UNKNOWN until that instrument is retrieved.
- **topics**: fees, inference, subsidiary-legislation, research-path
