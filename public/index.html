How the grounding works

Every statement in this game carries one of four labels, and the label is the point.

  • VERIFIED supported by a quoted source you can open. The deadlines in this mission are real Kenyan law.
  • INFERRED a conclusion drawn from sources, but not itself stated in them.
  • UNKNOWN the record does not answer this. Notably, the registration fee is UNKNOWN. The law authorises the charge and delegates the amount to rules that are not in this knowledge base, and the open web disagrees with itself by a factor of seven.
  • SIMULATION invented for the game — prices, queues, weather, customers. Never a claim about Kenya.

The Game Master can only choose between outcomes the engine has already computed. It cannot set a price, invent a requirement, or change your balance. That is enforced in the type signatures, not in a prompt.
